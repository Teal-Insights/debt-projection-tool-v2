# FX-share dataset QA audit — 23 July 2026

Full-coverage independent audit of the DSA foreign-currency-share dataset
shipped by the Debt Projection Tool v2, triggered by a user-reported
mismatch between displayed values and cited source pages (Albania,
Nigeria). Every one of the 167 sourced values was re-verified against its
cited IMF report page. Linear: TEA-880 (integration), following TEA-877
(bulk extraction), TEA-878 (human adjudication), TEA-879 (publication
candidate).

## Bottom line

161 of 167 values reproduced from their cited pages within tolerance
(83 exactly from printed numbers, 78 chart-derived within measurement
tolerance). 6 values were corrected. 7 rows had correct values but stale
citations, now repaired. The user-visible root cause was a transparency
gap, not (mainly) a data problem: the tool displayed a derived ratio while
the cited page shows only its ingredients, and the arithmetic connecting
them was recorded in an internal notes field that the UI never surfaced.

## Method (three layers)

1. **Deterministic layer** (`layer-a-deterministic-checks.py`, now
   institutionalized in `scripts/build-fx-share.mjs` as a build gate):
   parse the adjudicated dataset's own visible-arithmetic notes, recompute
   every ratio, and check metadata invariants (units, proxy/basis
   consistency, page fields, confidence caps) plus a WEO
   general-government-debt sanity band on denominators.
2. **Independent visual layer**: every cited PDF page (plus supporting
   pages referenced in the extraction notes) was re-rendered at scale 2.2
   from the sha-verified source PDFs and independently re-read by 12
   parallel auditor agents instructed to extract first and compare second,
   with axis-calibrated pixel measurement for chart-sourced values. All
   167 countries covered; every DISCREPANCY and CANNOT_VERIFY was then
   re-examined case by case against the page images, and the resulting
   corrections and policy calls were reviewed and approved by Teal Emery.
   No claim is made of a row-by-row human read of all 167 reports.
3. **Provenance layer**: sha256 chain from bundled CSV to the adjudicated
   package (TEA-878 final, row-identical to TEA-879
   `dataset-final.csv`), and from the QA overlay to the CSV; PDF hashes
   reconciled against the extraction archive.

Verdicts: `fx-share-audit-verdicts-2026-07-23.json` (pre-correction).
Page renders: `tea-877-20260717-30pzVs` staging archive (Mac Mini) and
`_tmp-fxshare-qa/pages/` (Drive, temporary).

## Provenance repairs (values unchanged, citations fixed)

AFG, BGD, BRA, EGY, PAK, ZAF, ZMB: the repo had bundled an intermediate
TEA-878 draft whose cited pages were one page off and whose `pdf_sha256`

## Policy decisions (owner: Teal, 23 July 2026)

- **Year policy**: latest available eligible observation, actual or staff
  estimate, labeled per country; projection-year values are never used as
  observations. 11 rows carry `yearStatus: estimate` (ATG, IRQ, MHL, MMR,
  NGA, NPL, SOM, STP, SYC, TZA, VUT); where the report also prints a hard
  actual, the implied alternative is disclosed (ATG 39.0, MHL 100.0,
  MMR 38.1, NGA 48.3, STP 48.0, TZA 64.7, VUT 78.2).
- **Figure precision**: chart-derived values display as approximate
  integers (≈42%), values under 0.75 as "<1%"; table/text values keep one
  decimal.
- **Derivation transparency**: the slider footnote now shows the exact
  arithmetic (numerator ÷ denominator in percent of GDP, a direct quote,
  or the chart-read description) for every sourced value, plus the audit
  outcome.

## Residual caveats (accepted, not blocking)

- GHA, LKA: the cited PDF revisions are not in the local extraction
  archive (values verified against same-title archived copies whose
  pagination matches the citations). Recommend re-archiving the exact
  files.
- SYC: `table_ref` names the Debt-by-Currency figure while the cited page
  carries the verifying text statement (value confirmed, printed 54).
- TZA: the source page's own "Domestic" share cell (39.8) is inconsistent
  with its levels (34.1); the external row used here is internally
  consistent.
- STP: report warns the currency criterion would differ significantly
  from the residency split; row is flagged as the weakest proxy.
- MHL: USD is legal tender, so the 100% "foreign currency" share is
  definitionally degenerate; kept with the residency-basis disclosure.
- Sub-0.3pp rounding notes on ARE, BTN, NRU, VCT (report-side rounding;
  values match the printed figures).

## How to re-run

- Deterministic gate: `npm run build:fx-share` (fails the build on any
  arithmetic or hash mismatch).
- Full visual audit: re-render cited pages from the archived PDFs and
  repeat the independent re-read (protocol above; ~1 person-day with
  agent assistance).
